PicoD64 front panel demo

The case and knob are rendered from the real 3D model, at about life size on a typical laptop screen. The display runs the firmware's own fw/ui.c, so these are the pixels the board would send to its OLED. The SD card is an invented sample.

Keyboard: ← → or ↑ ↓ turn, Space pushes, and holding it for 0.7 s is a long press. Mouse: scroll on the knob or drag its edge to turn; press its centre to push.

The whole case

About 106 × 91 mm. Two IEC sockets and USB are at the rear, and the microSD slot is on the front edge. Colours are one example print; yours depend on your filament.

Try

  1. Open Games, insert Starfall (Disk 1 of 3), then hold the knob to go to the next disk.
  2. Open Collection to feel a long list. In Games, rest on the long adventure name and watch it slide.
  3. Turn back one click from the top of the list to reach Settings, with Sound and New disk.
  4. Leave the list for 15 s: it returns to the status screen.

Board

Power cycling keeps the device number, the sound setting and any new disks. Reloading the page starts again. ROM and font changes apply at the next power cycle.

Log

Not simulated: the C64 and its browser, sound, the ACT light, the knob's feel and which way round it decodes, and the optional Commodore font from /chargen.bin. The OLED's colour depends on the module you bought.
