PicoD64 card setup

PicoD64 runs the 1541's own operating system, so it needs a copy of the drive's ROM on its SD card. The ROM is Commodore's copyright, so it can't come with PicoD64: this page fetches it for you from the zimmers.net archive, or uses a copy you already have, checks it, and saves it for the card. Nothing to install.

1The 1541 ROM

Get it from the archive. It comes in two halves; they are joined and checked here.

Or drop your own file(s) here: the 16 KB ROM, or its two 8 KB halves.

2The Commodore font (optional)

The OLED comes with a C64-style font. For the C64's own letters instead (wider: 14 characters a line instead of 19), add the C64's character ROM.

Or drop your own copy here (4 KB).

3Onto the card

    Put the card in this computer first. The files go in the card's top folder, next to your folders of games: not inside one.

    What exactly does this do?

    It downloads 1541-c000.325302-01.bin and 1541-e000.901229-05.bin (8 KB each) from zimmers.net, joins them into dos1541.bin (16,384 bytes) and checks its MD5 is a0ce8439d1b8dcf2e1430461f7233a72, the ROM PicoD64 is tested with. The font is characters.901225-01.bin, saved as chargen.bin. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

    Your own files are recognised by their contents, not their names. A 16 KB ROM that isn't the tested one (a JiffyDOS 1541 ROM, or another version) is accepted with a warning: it may work, but it hasn't been tested. JiffyDOS needs the JiffyDOS KERNAL in the C64 too.

    The same, from a command line: fw/tools/get-roms.sh (Linux, macOS) or get-roms.ps1 (Windows).
