## Fetched when needed, not included

| Item | Used for | Terms |

[`hardware/lib/3d/fetch.sh`](hardware/lib/3d/fetch.sh) downloads the Lumberg and Alps models and checks them by SHA-256; the GCT model is downloaded by hand ([`hardware/lib/3d/README.md`](hardware/lib/3d/README.md)) |
| Eurostile font | The case's lettering | Commercial. [`case/gen_glyphs.sh`](case/gen_glyphs.sh) sets the case up for another font (README) |
