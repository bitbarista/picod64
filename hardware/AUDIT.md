The rule (2026-10-25): never trust the process. Every aspect of this design is checked against primary
sources, not against the tools that produced it.

**Rule for this file:** an item counts as checked only when it is compared against a **primary
source** (the maker's drawing or datasheet, the C64 or Pico documents). My generators, KiCad's
library or a passing ERC/DRC don't count. Each line names its source.
Status: ✅ checked against source · ❌ wrong, fixed (see note) · ⚠ open · ⏳ not yet done.
